# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Book Recommendation System project welcomes respectful participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. We aim to keep discussions constructive and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Critique code and ideas without attacking the people who shared them.
- Accept good-faith feedback and make room for different viewpoints.
- Use inclusive language and respect people's identities and boundaries.
- Share only data, images, and other material that you have permission to disclose.
- Keep personal information, credentials, and private dataset records out of public discussions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, persistent disruption, doxxing, retaliation, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberately sharing credentials or personal records is also prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces used to discuss or maintain this project.

Report conduct concerns privately to maintainer Brajesh Kumar through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/brajesh31). Include a factual description and context; do not put private details in a public issue. For suspected vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review reports and respond as time permits. Responses may include a request to stop, a warning, content removal, temporary restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces. Privacy will be protected as far as reasonably possible. Retaliation against someone raising a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
